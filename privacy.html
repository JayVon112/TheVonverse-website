Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 28, 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy explains how The Vonverse and JayVon AI may collect, process, use, and protect information when you use JayVon AI and related Vonverse services.

By using JayVon AI, you acknowledge the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

2. Information We May Process

Depending on the features enabled in a Discord server, JayVon AI may process information such as:

  • Discord user IDs.
  • Discord server IDs.
  • Usernames and display names.
  • Server configuration and settings.
  • Bot commands and interactions.
  • Moderation-related information.
  • Security and diagnostic logs.
  • Information required for specific JayVon AI features.

3. AI Conversations

When you interact with JayVon AI, information contained in your interaction may be processed to generate an AI response.

Depending on the feature being used, conversation information may be temporarily processed or stored in order to provide the requested functionality.

Please do not provide passwords, payment information, private credentials, or other highly sensitive information to JayVon AI.

4. How Information Is Used

Information may be used to:

  • Operate and maintain JayVon AI.
  • Provide requested features.
  • Maintain server settings.
  • Provide moderation and community tools.
  • Detect and prevent abuse.
  • Diagnose technical problems.
  • Improve reliability and performance.

5. Information Sharing

We do not intend to sell personal information to third parties.

Information may be processed by third-party services that are necessary to operate JayVon AI, such as hosting providers, infrastructure providers, or AI service providers.

6. Data Retention

We aim to retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary to operate JayVon AI, maintain requested features, resolve technical issues, prevent abuse, or meet legitimate operational requirements.

Retention periods may vary depending on the type of information and the feature involved.

7. Data Deletion Requests

If you believe JayVon AI has stored information associated with you that should be removed, you may contact The Vonverse development team through the official support channels.

We may request information necessary to identify the relevant account, server, or data before processing a deletion request.

8. Discord

JayVon AI operates through Discord and may interact with Discord's APIs and services.

Discord independently handles information according to its own policies. JayVon AI is not owned, operated, or officially endorsed by Discord.

9. Security

We use reasonable measures intended to protect information processed by JayVon AI.

However, no internet-based service can guarantee absolute security.

10. Children's Privacy

JayVon AI is not intended to knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable laws.

11. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated as JayVon AI and The Vonverse develop.

When significant changes are made, the "Last Updated" date will be updated accordingly.

12. Contact

If you have questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy or information handled by JayVon AI, please contact The Vonverse development team through the official Vonverse support channels.
