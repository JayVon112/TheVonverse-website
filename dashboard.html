Control music permissions and who can instantly pause playback.

Music Controls

Configure JayVon's music system.

Music System Enable music commands.
Queue Control Control who can modify the queue.
Instant Pause Permissions Only selected roles can instantly pause another user's music session.
⏸️ Roles That Can Pause Instantly Select the Discord role allowed to instantly pause.
!pause Pause the current music playback.
!pause 1 Pause playback slot 1.
!pause 2 Pause playback slot 2.

Members without the selected role will not be allowed to instantly pause another user's playback. The real permission check will be handled by the Discord backend when it is connected.
